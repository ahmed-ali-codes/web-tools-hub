Common Percentage Examples

Discounts

"What's the final price after a 20% discount on a $50 item?"

50 - (20% × 50) = $40

Tips

"How much is a 15% tip on a $75 restaurant bill?"

15% × 75 = $11.25

Taxes

"What's the total with 8% sales tax on a $120 purchase?"

120 + (8% × 120) = $129.60

Growth

"A $1,000 investment grew to $1,200. What's the percentage increase?"

(1200 - 1000) ÷ 1000 × 100 = 20%
