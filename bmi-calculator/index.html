Health & Wellness Tips

Balanced Diet

Eat a variety of foods including fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and whole grains to maintain a healthy weight.

Regular Exercise

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity or 75 minutes of vigorous activity each week.

Hydration

Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Limit sugary drinks and excessive caffeine.

Quality Sleep

Adults should aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night to support overall health.
